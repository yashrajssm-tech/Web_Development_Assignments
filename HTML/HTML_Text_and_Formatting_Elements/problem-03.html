CJP won, Education Minister resigned

Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan have resigned after continuous protest on Jantar Mantar

It is said the the Gen-Z is very strong generation, yesterday it is proved. Education Minister, Dharmendra Pradhan have resigned after continuous protest on Jnatar Mantar, in Delhi.
It should be noted that protest was continuously going on Jantar Mantar, Delhi and also in various other cities across the country because of the NEET paper leak and also several other government exams paper leaks. It should be noted that due to this paper leak almost 20 students commited suicide across the country.
The most prominent mobilisation began in June with the youth-led Cockroach Janta Party (CJP) and student organisations protesting alleged irregularities surrounding the 2026 NEET examination and other national examinations. Protesters demanded accountability from the Union government, reforms to the examination system, compensation for families of students who died by suicide amid the examination controversy, and the resignation of Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The agitation continued for weeks and grew into a nationwide youth movement.
Moreover, famous personality Sonam Wangchuk had even joined the protest by doing hunger strike. He was on his hunger strike for almost 26 days.
Police force chose brutality to supress the protest they used lathi-charge, tear gases and even pellet guns against the crowd. Many people were injured in the encounter, also many people were arrested alleged. But yesterday the protest became successful when Dharmendra Pradhan gave his resignation letter to the honourable President Shrimati Draupadi Murmu. Shri Prahlad Joshi has been appointed as the new Education Minister, who will oath for the position within few days.
