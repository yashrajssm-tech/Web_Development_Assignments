About University

GLA University is an esteemed private institution in the city of Mathura with an off-campus in Greater Noida. The institute was established by Narayan Das Agrawal in 1998. The University has a NAAC A+ Accredation, is approved by AICTE and has a status of 12-B by UGC.
The main campus is located at Mathura with a lush-green campus area of 110 acres and 20 acres sports facility, also University has various clubs which students can join so that they can enhance their inner talents, have a look at real-life based problems and also find the solution to them. GLA University has an extraordinary track record in terms of placements also. The placement rate in University is 86% in last 5 years. The college have 850+ faculty members from global institutions who provide quality education. GLA has a strong alumni network with 51000+ alumni who are settled across globe.
Recently, in 2024, a campus in Greater Noida was also opened with an ambition of industry-oriented education. The university also offers different online couses. Probably, it is all these qualities because of which the institution has an enrollment of 24,000+ students across the Mathura campus, Noida Campus and online courses.

Campus Facilities

Mathura Campus

GLA University, Mathura, is vibrant and enjoyable, offering students a balance of academics, activities, and social experiences. The university provides a pleasant environment with modern facilities, sports areas, clubs, cultural events, and opportunities for students to develop their skills beyond the classroom. Students can participate in various competitions, workshops, festivals, and extracurricular activities while making new friends and making memorable experinces.
The campus has separate hostels for boys and girls. A large 20 acre sports areas having Cricket ground, basketball court, Badminton court,etc. There are 70+ clubs in the campus and even a shopping mall.

Greater Noida campus

The Greater Noida campus is under construction the current building has 6 floors and 2 basement which comprises of parking and a small Cafeteria. The hostel is almost 2.5 kms away from institute, bus services are available. Institute has very well smart classrooms , dedicated labs and also several clubs. Orientation programs are organsied for freshers in which they do various fun activites and even got to know some useful knowledge. There is a library in the campus and also a play area which has pool area, carrom-board, table tennis, etc.
