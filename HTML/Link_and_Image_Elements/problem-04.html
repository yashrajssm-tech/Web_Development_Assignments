Agra Travel Destination Guide

Welcome to Agra, one of India's most famous historical destinations. Agra is located in Uttar Pradesh and is best known for the magnificent Taj Mahal. It is an excellent destination for a weekend trip.

1. Top Attractions

Taj Mahal

The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in the world. It was built by Mughal emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal. The monument is famous for its beautiful white marble architecture.

Learn more about the Taj Mahal on the UNESCO website

Agra Fort

Agra Fort is a large historical fort built mainly from red sandstone. It contains beautiful palaces, halls and courtyards associated with the Mughal emperors.

Visit the UNESCO page for Agra Fort

Fatehpur Sikri

Fatehpur Sikri is a historic Mughal city located near Agra. It is famous for its impressive buildings, courtyards and gateways.

2. Culture and Heritage

Agra has a rich cultural heritage influenced by the Mughal period. The city is famous for traditional handicrafts, marble inlay work, carpets and leather products. Visitors can explore local markets and experience traditional Indian culture. Agra's historical buildings also provide an opportunity to learn about Mughal architecture and history.

3. Famous Food of Agra

Agra offers a variety of delicious North Indian and Mughlai foods. The city is especially famous for its traditional sweet called Agra Petha.

4. Best Time to Visit

Agra can be visited throughout the year, but the weather is generally more comfortable during the cooler months. October to March is the recommended season for visiting Agra. The summer months can be very hot, while the monsoon season can bring rain and humidity. The cooler season is more comfortable for sightseeing.

5. Travel Tips

Important travel tip: Start your sightseeing early in the morning to avoid the strongest heat and large crowds at popular attractions.


Wear comfortable shoes because sightseeing involves walking.
Carry drinking water when visiting monuments.
Keep your valuables safe in crowded places.
Check monument timings before visiting.
Respect local customs and historical monuments.
Keep the tourist attractions clean.

6. How to Reach Agra

Agra can be reached by road, railway and air. It has good railway connections with major cities such as Delhi, Agra Cant and Agra Fort are the major railway stations. Road travel is also convenient from Delhi and nearby cities. Kheria Airport is the airport in Agra.
For a weekend trip from Delhi, travelling by train or road can be a convenient option.
