Understanding Interest Rates

An interest rate is the cost of borrowing money or the return earned on money deposited with a financial institution. It may be expressed as an annual percentage rate.

For example, if the principal is ₹100,000 and the annual interest rate is 8%, simple annual interest would be:

Interest = Principal × Rate × Time

= ₹100,000 × 8% × 1 = ₹8,000

Educational Note

Actual loan interest can be calculated using reducing-balance, compound-interest or other methods depending on the product. Always check the lender's official terms.

What Is EMI?

EMI is the periodic payment made toward a loan. It generally contains both a principal component and an interest component.

A commonly used EMI formula is:

EMI = P × r × (1 + r)n ÷ ((1 + r)n − 1)

Interest Rate
The percentage charged on the outstanding loan amount.
EMI
The periodic instalment paid toward the loan.
Principal
The original amount borrowed, before interest and charges.
Tenure
The period over which the loan is scheduled to be repaid.

Financial Tips Before Borrowing

  • Compare the total cost of borrowing, not just the advertised rate.
  • Check processing fees and other applicable charges.
  • Keep an emergency fund before taking on significant debt.
  • Choose a repayment period that fits your budget.
  • Read the loan agreement and repayment schedule carefully.
  • Check whether prepayment or foreclosure charges apply.
  • Avoid borrowing more than you reasonably need.

How to Prepare for a Financial Product

Before applying, organize your identity, income and financial documents. Different financial institutions have different eligibility criteria.

  1. Identify the financial product you need.
  2. Compare rates, fees, benefits and repayment terms.
  3. Review eligibility requirements.
  4. Prepare the required identity and income documents.
  5. Submit the application through the institution's official channel.
  6. Read the approved terms before accepting the product.
  7. Keep copies of the agreement and repayment schedule.
