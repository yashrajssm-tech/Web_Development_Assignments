About the Event

Discover Your Next Opportunity

The FutureTech Career & Innovation Expo 2026 is a two-day technology and career event designed to connect ambitious students, graduates, job seekers, entrepreneurs and experienced professionals with leading organizations.

Attendees can explore career opportunities, internship programs, placement drives, workshops, certifications and professional networking opportunities. The event covers artificial intelligence, software engineering, cybersecurity, cloud computing, data science, fintech, product management and emerging technologies.

“The future belongs to people who continuously learn, collaborate and turn knowledge into meaningful action.”

— FutureTech Expo Organizing Committee

How to Prepare

Preparation Tips

  • Update your resume before attending.
  • Carry several printed copies of your resume.
  • Prepare a short professional introduction.
  • Research organizations you want to meet.
  • Prepare technical and career-related questions.
  • Bring a notebook or digital note-taking device.
  • Review your core technical subjects.
  • Dress appropriately for professional interactions.
  • Keep your LinkedIn profile updated.
  • Arrive at least 30 minutes before your first session.

Your Attendee Journey

From Registration to Opportunity

  1. Register online using the official registration form.
  2. Receive your event confirmation.
  3. Check in at the registration desk.
  4. Collect your attendee badge and event guide.
  5. Attend the opening keynote.
  6. Visit company exhibition booths.
  7. Join workshops and expert sessions.
  8. Meet recruiters and industry professionals.
  9. Submit your resume where relevant.
  10. Attend networking and closing sessions.
