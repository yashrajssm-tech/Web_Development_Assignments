Destination Overview

Agra, located in Uttar Pradesh, India, is world‑famous for the Taj Mahal — a UNESCO World Heritage Site and symbol of eternal love ♥. The city is rich in Mughal architecture, vibrant bazaars, and cultural heritage.

Attractions

  • Taj Mahal
  • Agra Fort
  • Mehtab Bagh
  • Fatehpur Sikri
  • Itimad‑ud‑Daulah (Baby Taj)
  • Akbar’s Tomb
  • Jama Masjid
  • Kinari Bazaar

Travel Tips

  1. Start early to catch the Taj Mahal at sunrise.
  2. Carry water and sunscreen .
  3. Use local guides for deeper insights.
  4. Try Mughlai cuisine at local eateries.
  5. Respect cultural norms and dress modestly.
