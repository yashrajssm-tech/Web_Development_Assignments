Welcome to Kailash Hospital

Kailash Hospital provides accessible and patient-centered healthcare services for individuals and families. Our team includes experienced doctors, nurses, technicians, and support.
We focus on respectful communication, convenient appointments, preventive care, and coordinated services.
Our goal is to provide a comfortable environment where patients can discuss their healthcare concerns with qualified professionals.

"Good healthcare begins with listening to the patient and treating every person with dignity."
— Dr. Ananya Mehta, Medical Director

Appointments are recommended for specialist consultations, although selected OPD services may accept walk-in patients.

Patient Resources

Before visiting the clinic, patients should review the following information.

Patient Instructions

  • Bring a valid identification document.
  • Bring previous medical reports when relevant.
  • Arrive approximately 10 minutes before your appointment.
  • Carry a list of current medicines if applicable.
  • Inform reception about accessibility requirements.
  • Follow staff instructions while using clinic facilities.

Appointment Steps

  1. Select your preferred department.
  2. Choose a doctor or consultation type.
  3. Submit the appointment request form.
  4. Wait for confirmation from the clinic.
  5. Arrive at the clinic at the confirmed time.

Healthcare Terms

Consultation
A scheduled discussion between a patient and a healthcare professional about the patient's healthcare needs.
Follow-up
A subsequent consultation intended to review progress or continue an existing care plan.
OPD
Outpatient Department, where patients receive healthcare services without being admitted to the clinic or hospital.
Appointment
A scheduled time reserved for a patient consultation or service.
