Event Overview

The FuturePath Career & Opportunity Fair 2026 is a two-day career development event designed for students, graduates, job seekers and early-career professionals.

Participants will have the opportunity to meet recruiters, explore internships, placements, scholarships and apprenticeships, attend expert sessions and improve their employability skills.

The event brings together organizations from IT, finance, consulting, engineering, education and social-impact sectors.

Objectives of the Event

  • Connect participants with credible organizations.
  • Introduce students to emerging career opportunities.
  • Provide practical resume and interview guidance.
  • Promote internships and industry exposure.
  • Explain eligibility requirements for opportunities.
  • Encourage professional networking.

Who Should Attend?

The event is suitable for college students, final-year students, recent graduates, diploma holders, career changers and early-career professionals.

"Our goal is not simply to help participants find a job. We want every attendee to leave with a clearer understanding of their strengths, opportunities and next career step."

— FuturePath Organizing Committee

General event registration is free. Some workshops may have limited seating.

Preparation Guide

Important Notice

Bring a valid photo ID, copies of your resume, a notebook and your registration confirmation.

Preparation Tips

  • Update your resume before attending.
  • Check your resume for spelling errors.
  • Prepare a 30-second professional introduction.
  • Research participating organizations.
  • Prepare questions for recruiters.
  • Carry printed copies of your resume.
  • Keep a digital copy of your resume.
  • Dress appropriately for recruiter interactions.

Attendee Journey

  1. Complete online registration.
  2. Receive your registration confirmation.
  3. Arrive at the venue.
  4. Complete event check-in.
  5. Attend your selected sessions.
  6. Visit organization booths.
  7. Speak with recruiters.
  8. Follow up after the event.

Career Opportunity Glossary

Career Fair
An event where candidates meet employers and explore different career opportunities.
Internship
A short-term professional learning opportunity that provides practical workplace experience.
Placement
A recruitment opportunity that can lead to full-time employment.
Scholarship
Financial assistance provided to eligible students for education or training.
Apprenticeship
A structured learning opportunity combining practical workplace training and formal instruction.
