Hiring Process

Our hiring process is designed to be fair, structured, and transparent.

  1. Application Review
  2. Recruiter Screening
  3. Technical or Role-Specific Round
  4. Panel Interview
  5. HR Discussion
  6. Offer

Hiring Stage Details

Screening
A recruiter reviews your resume and application against the requirements of the position.
Technical Round
Depending on the position, candidates may complete coding, system design, portfolio, case-study, or technical questions.
HR Round
The HR team discusses your goals, communication style, availability, expectations, and company culture.
Offer
Successful candidates receive an employment offer containing information about compensation, benefits, and joining details.
