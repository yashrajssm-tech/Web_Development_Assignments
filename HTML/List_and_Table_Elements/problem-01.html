Admission Overview

Welcome! to our admissions portal. We are committed to making the admission process simple, transparent, and accessible for every student. Our goal is to help prospective students understand the eligibility requirements, application process, important dates, and documents required for admission.

Eligility

Undergraduate courses
Candidates seeking admission to undergraduate programs at GLA University must generally have completed their 10+2 (Class 12) or an equivalent examination from a recognized board.
Post-Graduate courses
For admission to postgraduate programs, candidates must hold a relevant bachelor's degree from a recognized university or institution. The minimum marks and subject requirements depend on the specific postgraduate course.
Ph. D. courses
Candidates applying for Ph.D. programs generally need a master's degree or equivalent qualification in a relevant discipline from a recognized university, along with the minimum marks prescribed by the university. Additional entrance tests and interviews may be part of the selection process

Application Process

The admission process at GLA University is designed to provide students with a simple and structured way to apply for their preferred undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral programs.

  1. Register Online

    2. Eligible candidates can begin the admission process by registering through the official GLA University admission portal. Applicants need to provide basic personal and academic information and create their application credentials.

  2. Complete the application form

    3. After registration, candidates need to fill out the application form with accurate details, including personal information, educational qualifications, contact details, and course preferences.

  3. Upload required documents

    4. Applicants may be required to upload scanned copies of relevant documents,

  4. Pay the Application Fee

    5. Candidates must pay the applicable application fee through the available online payment options. The application form should be submitted only after ensuring that all required information and documents have been provided correctly.

  5. Entrance Examination or Selection

    6. Depending on the program, admission may be based on academic merit, entrance examination performance, or other selection criteria prescribed by GLA University.

  6. Admission Confirmation

    7. Shortlisted candidates may receive an admission offer from the university.

  7. Fee Payment and Enrollment

    8. After selection, candidates need to pay the prescribed admission/academic fees to confirm their seat. Once the required formalities are completed, the student can proceed with enrollment and begin their academic journey at GLA University.

Required Documents

Applicants may be required to upload scanned copies of relevant documents,

  • Recent passport size photo
  • Class 10 and 12 marksheets
  • Graduation marksheet or degree certificate, where applicable
  • Valid identity proof

Important term

Students applying to GLA University must fulfill the eligibility criteria and provide accurate information and valid documents during the admission process. Admission and fee payments must be completed within the specified deadlines, and applicable refund policies will follow university rules.
Students are also expected to follow the university's academic, attendance, examination, and disciplinary regulations. Admission criteria, fees, deadlines, and other policies may vary by course and academic session, so candidates should always check the latest official information before applying.
