Academic Rules

  • Students must maintain the required attendance percentage.
  • Students must arrive on time for lectures and practical classes.
  • Mobile phones must be kept silent during academic sessions.
  • Students must carry their identity card on campus.
  • Laboratory equipment must be handled carefully.
  • Assignments and practical records must be submitted before the deadline.
  • Students must maintain discipline and respect faculty and classmates.

Academic Terminology

Lecture
A classroom session in which theoretical concepts are taught.
Lab
A practical session where students apply concepts using equipment or software.
Tutorial
A guided academic session focused on problem-solving and discussion.
Internal Assessment
Evaluation conducted during the semester through tests, assignments, presentations or other academic activities.
Practical
A hands-on academic activity in which students perform experiments or implement concepts.
