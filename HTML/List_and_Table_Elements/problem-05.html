Annual College Fest 2026

Three Days of Fun, Talent, Competition and Celebration

Fest Introduction

Annual College Fest 2026 is a three-day celebration of creativity, talent, sports, technology and cultural activities. Students from different colleges are invited to participate and showcase their skills.
Venue: In Front of AB-I |
Dates: 15–17 April 2026

Registration Process

Students can register online through the official registration portal. Registration Deadline: 10 April 2026

  1. Visit the official registration portal.
  2. Create an account using your college email address.
  3. Select the competition you want to participate in.
  4. Enter your personal and college details.
  5. Upload the required documents.
  6. Pay the registration fee, if applicable.
  7. Submit the registration form.
  8. Save your registration/reference number.

Rules

  • Participants must carry a valid college ID card.
  • Participants must report at the venue at least 30 minutes before the event.
  • Participants must follow the instructions of the coordinators.
  • Any form of cheating or misconduct may result in disqualification.
  • Participants are responsible for their personal belongings.
  • Decisions of the judges and organizing committee will be final.
  • Maintain cleanliness and discipline throughout the fest.

Fest Terms and Definitions

Open Event
An event in which students can participate without being restricted to a specific course or group.
Team Event
An event where two or more participants compete together as a team.
Individual Event
An event where a single participant competes independently.
Registration
The process of officially enrolling for an event or competition.
Coordinator
A person responsible for organizing and managing a particular event.
Final Round
The last stage of a competition in which selected participants compete for the winning position.
