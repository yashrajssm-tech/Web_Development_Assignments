Buying Factors of a laptop

  • RAM

  • Processor
    • - The processor decides how fast a computer will perform a task so a good processor is very important.
  • Storage
    • - It is used to store data. It should be chose by a person as per his/her needs.
  • Display
    • - Now-a-days it is recommended to buy a hotel having Full HD+ display or OLED display with a good refesh rate.
  • Battery Life
    • - It is very nessary factor while buying a laptop as it decides for how much time will the laptop work.
  • Warrenty
    • - A very important factor which works in case a laptop gets damaged.

How to buy a proper laptop for me?

  1. Decide your purpose
    2. - Choose whether you need it for study, office work, gaming, programming, or video editing.
  2. Set your budget
    3. - Decide how much you can comfortably spend.
  3. Choose Specifications you require
    4. - Prefer 16 GB RAM, 512 GB SSD, and a recent-generation processor for most users. If you are buying laptop for gaming and editing purpose note that your computer should have a proper graphic-card installed in it.
  4. Check the display and battery
    5. - Look for a good-quality screen and long battery life.
  5. Compare different laptops
    6. - Compare performance, price, reviews, warranty, and features before buying.
    COMMON Mistake
    - People generally buy gaming laptop due to their high performance even if they do not require such Specifications. Gaming laptop has a short batery life as compared to other laptops, so but it only if it is required!

Some General terms

RAM
RAM is the laptop's temporary memory. More RAM helps you run multiple programs smoothly.
SSD
SSD is storage that saves your files, apps, and operating system. It makes the laptop start and load programs quickly.
CPU
CPU is the main processor that performs calculations and runs programs.
GPU
GPU handles graphics and is especially important for gaming, video editing, and 3D applications.
Refresh Rate
Refresh rate is how many times the screen updates per second, measured in Hz. A higher rate makes motion look smoother.
Battery Capacity
Battery capacity indicates how much energy the battery can store, usually measured in Wh (watt-hours).
