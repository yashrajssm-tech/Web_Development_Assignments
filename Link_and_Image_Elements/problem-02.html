CJP won, Education Minister resigned

Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan have resigned after continuous protest on Jantar Mantar

What

The protest was an agitation against the alleged NEET-UG 2026 paper leak and irregularities in Government competitive examinations. It was largely driven by students and young people and was organised prominently by the Cockroach Janta Party (CJP), founded by Abhijeet Dipke. The protesters demanded accountability, a transparent examination system and the resignation of Union Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The issue attracted wider public attention when activist Sonam Wangchuk joined the agitation and began an indefinite hunger strike.

Where

The major protest took place at Jantar Mantar in New Delhi, one of the country's designated places for demonstrations. Hundreds of students, young people and supporters gathered there. The protest later attracted political and social figures, including Sonam Wangchuk, while leaders such as M. A. Baby and Brinda Karat also visited the protest site.

When

The CJP-led protest movement began at Jantar Mantar on 6 June 2026, while the continuing protest was formally being held from 20 June amid the NEET paper-leak controversy. On 28 June, Sonam Wangchuk joined the protest and began an indefinite hunger strike, giving the movement considerable national attention. Rahul Gandhi later expressed support for Wangchuk and criticised his removal from Jantar Mantar.

Why

The main reason for the protest was the demand for accountability over the alleged NEET-UG paper leak and other examination irregularities. Protesters argued that such incidents undermine the fairness of competitive examinations and put honest students at a disadvantage. They demanded strict action against those responsible, reforms to strengthen examination security and transparency, and the resignation of Education Minister Dharmendra Pradhan. The participation of prominent figures such as Sonam Wangchuk, along with support from political leaders including Rahul Gandhi, helped bring greater national attention to the students' concerns.
