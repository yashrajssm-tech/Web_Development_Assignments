GLA University

About the College

GLA University is an esteemed private institution in the city of Mathura with an off-campus in Greater Noida. The institute was established by Narayan Das Agrawal in 1998. The University has a NAAC A+ Accredation, is approved by AICTE and has a status of 12-B by UGC.
The main Campus is located at Mathura with a lush-green campus area of 110 acres and 20 acres sports facility, also University has various clubs which students can join so that they can enhancce their inner talents, have a look at real-life based problems and also find the solution to them. GLA University has an extraordinary track record in terms of placements also. The placement rate in University is 86% in last 5 years. The college have 850+ faculty members from global institutions who provide quality education. GLA has a strong alumni network with 51000+ alumni who are settled across globe.
Recently, in 2024 a campus in Greater Noida was also opened with an ambition of industry-oriented education. The university also offers different online couses. Probably, it is all these qualities because of which the institution has an enrollment of 24,000+ students across the Mathura campus, Noida Campus and online courses.

Courses Offered

The institute provides various courses. Mathura campus has Graduate courses, Post-Graduate courses, Doctral programs and even Diploma courses.
Graduate Courses mainly has B.Tech in CSE, CSE(specialisation in AIML), ECE, EE, etc. BBA, BCA, B.Pharm, B.A, B.Sc, etc. Post-Graduate courses has M.Tech in CSE, ECE, etc, M.Pharm, MCA, MBA. M.Sc, M.A, etc. Doctral programs mainly has Ph.D and also Diploma courses.
Greater Noida has only several courses which are B.Tech in CSE(Core and AIML) and ECE, BBA and MCA in CSE
Online couses has BBA, MBA, B.com, etc

Campus life

Since, I am a student in Greater Noida campus. So, I will share about my campus life there. Campus here is not very large but has a building with 6 floors and 2 basement which comprises of parking and a small Cafeteria. Some of the college part is under construction. We have a hostel almost 2.5 kms away from institute so we got to intitute by bus. Institute has very well smart classrooms , dedicated labs and also several clubs. Recently, we a Orientation program too in which we did various fun activites an even got to know some useful knowledge.

Why I Like my College

I like my college because I have got everything in the college which I was expecting. The faculties are very good and help us in learning until we don't get that. Also, we learn almost everything practically with real-life experience not just theoretically.
