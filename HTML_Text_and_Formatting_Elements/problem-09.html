Student Terms & Rules

General Rules

Students must carry their valid college ID card at all times.
Students must maintain cleanliness and keep classrooms and campus areas tidy.
Everyone is responsible for maintaining a clean learning environment.
Students must follow the instructions given by faculty members and university authorities.

Attendance

Students must maintain at least 75% attendance in each subject.
Attendance below the required percentage may make a student ineligible to appear for the examination.
Medical or other approved leave may be considered according to university rules.

Examination

Students must report to the examination hall at least 15 minutes before the scheduled time.
Students must carry their valid ID card and required examination materials.
Any form of unfair means during an examination may result in cancellation of the examination and disciplinary action.

Assignment Submission

All assignments must be submitted through the prescribed method.
Assignments must be submitted by the deadline specified by the faculty member.
Candidates who didn't submit their assignments by the deadline will be declared absent.
Students should mention their name, roll number, course, and assignment title on their assignments.

Discipline


Students must behave respectfully with faculty members, staff, and fellow students.
Bullying, ragging, harassment, damaging university property, or disruptive behaviour may lead to disciplinary action.
Students are expected to maintain the reputation and dignity of the institution.

It should be noted that all the students must abide by the rules and regulations set by institution any misleading can lead to their suspension from college and even legal actions against them.
