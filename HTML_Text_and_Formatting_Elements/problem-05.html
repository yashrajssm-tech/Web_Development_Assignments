Physics

Some of the important information in physics

Newton's Law of Motion:

(i) First law of motion: An object at rest continue to be in rest, an object in motion continue to be motion, continue to be in motion unless an external force is applied to them. This is also called Law of Inertia
(ii) Second law of motion: Rate of change in momentum gives the force applied to the object.
Force = mass(velocityfinal - velocityinitial)/time
(iii) Third law of motion: To every action, there is equal and opposite reaction.

Unit of some of the quantities

(i) Momentum = kg m s-1
(ii) Force = Newton(N)
(iii) Volume = m3
(iv) Density = kg m-3
